# DavPanel Mobile App

DavPanel, in your pocket. Manage your DavPanel accounts and DavAdmin servers from your phone - add every server you run, check their status, and log in without typing a URL each time.

See it at [davpanel.com/mobile](https://davpanel.com/mobile/), including the download link, screenshots, and FAQ.

## Features

- **Multiple servers, one app** - Add every DavPanel account and DavAdmin server you manage, and switch between them instantly.
- **DavPanel & DavAdmin, both supported** - One app for both products - pick the type when you add a server, or let the app detect it from the port.
- **Credentials stay on your device** - Logins are stored in your phone's secure keychain, not on a third-party server.
- **See what's reachable at a glance** - Each saved server shows a live green or red status dot, so you know what needs attention before you open it.

## Getting started

1. Download the APK from [davpanel.com/mobile](https://davpanel.com/mobile/) on your phone.
2. Open the downloaded file - Android will warn it's from outside the Play Store the first time, tap "Settings" and allow installs from this source.
3. Open the app, tap "+ Add server", and enter your panel's URL, username and password.

Free & open source, requires Android 7.0 or newer.
