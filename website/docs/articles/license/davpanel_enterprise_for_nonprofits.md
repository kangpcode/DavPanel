# Discounted Licenses for Education and Nonprofits

How to get a discount for DavPanel licenses for educational or non-profit organizations:

Are there discounts on DavPanel Enterprise licenses for educational institutions or non-profit organizations?

Yes, we offer discounts for educational institutions and non-profit organizations. Please [reach out](mailto:info@davpanel.com) to us with the following details:

- Contact information, including the full address and the name of the contact person.
- The number of Enterprise licenses needed.
- The intended purpose for the licenses.
